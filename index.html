Why The Discovery Group

One partner for your organization’s defining moments.

We believe social profit organizations can change the world. Since 2017, our mission has been to help non-profits advance their purpose and strengthen community outcomes. Our home base is in Vancouver, BC, and we support clients across Canada and beyond.

  1. 01

    Only nonprofits, since 2017.

    We work exclusively with social profit organizations. Our team is made up of people who have walked a similar path as you, with hands-on experience from the non-profit sector.

  2. 02

    One partner for the whole inflection point.

    Campaign counsel, the leader to run it, the board to govern it, and coaching to sustain it: our integrated team means no hand-offs between firms.

  3. 03

    Thinking partners, not just consultants.

    We collaborate as you face significant moments of inflection, and look for ways to add value beyond what is typically expected in a consultant/client relationship.
