Finding The Right Pace As A New CEO With Brian Torrance, President & CEO Of ParticipACTION

September 15, 2026 · Hosted by Douglas Nelson

Stepping into a major leadership role is a thrilling milestone, but finding the right pace can be one of the greatest challenges for any incoming executive. Striking the balance between executing bold, decisive actions and taking the time to listen, learn, and absorb organizational history is crucial for long-term sustainability. In this episode, host Douglas Nelson sits down with Brian Torrance, the President and CEO of ParticipACTION, to discuss the delicate art of executive transition. Brian shares his personal reflections on taking the helm of an iconic Canadian national charity, managing board dynamics, and the critical importance of a “cadence check” to ensure the entire team is aligned. He also outlines how ParticipACTION is connecting with a new generation of Canadians through “new nostalgia” and family-centered physical activity initiatives.

Reintroducing An Iconic Brand Through “New Nostalgia”

My guest is Brian Torrance, President and CEO of ParticipACTION . Brian is a leader who is deeply committed to building a healthier and more active Canada. Through his work at ParticipACTION, he leads a team focused on helping Canadians of all ages find meaningful opportunities to move, connect, and improve their well-being in the places where they live and work.

In our conversation, Brian talks about energizing an iconic Canadian organization, and introducing it to a new generation of Canadians, as well as the process of embracing ParticipACTION’s roots as a charity. Brian’s vision, grace, and determination are all examples of leading well in turbulent times. Please enjoy my conversation with Brian Torrance.
