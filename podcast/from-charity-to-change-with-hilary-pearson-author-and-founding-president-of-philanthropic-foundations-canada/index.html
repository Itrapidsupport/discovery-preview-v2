From Charity To Change With Hilary Pearson, Author And Founding President Of Philanthropic Foundations Canada

September 15, 2026 · Hosted by Douglas Nelson

How do we move from simple, transactional giving to creating real, lasting change in our communities? The answer lies in the shift toward relational philanthropy, a concept that is redefining how modern foundations operate and support their partners. Host Douglas Nelson is joined by Hilary Pearson, the founding president of Philanthropic Foundations Canada and author of the 2022 book From Charity to Change: Inside the World of Canadian Foundations. In this episode, Hilary unpacks the transition from traditional, top-down funding models to a collaborative approach built on trust, shared learning, and deep community partnerships. They explore the unique role of foundations as catalysts and conveners, the generational shift in sector leadership, and how working together differently can solve complex social issues. Discover how shifting the focus to deeper relationships can elevate your organization’s impact.
