Trust Before Dollars: Leadership Lessons With Allison Young, CEO, KGH Foundation

September 23, 2026 · Hosted by Douglas Nelson

Healthcare is changing rapidly, demanding that organizations look past traditional fundraising to build deep, trust-based community relationships. At the forefront of this shift is the KGH Foundation, an organization redefining how healthcare philanthropy operates both inside and outside hospital walls. In this conversation, host Douglas Nelson sits down with Allison Young, the outgoing CEO of KGH Foundation, to explore her decade of transformative leadership. Allison shares her insights on challenging long-held assumptions about hospital foundations and how to lead with courage when pioneering new collaborative models. She also discusses the creation of Thrive Okanagan and the revolutionary Jim Pattison Centre for Health Systems Learning and Innovation, demonstrating what is possible when community generosity meets bold system change.

Redefining Healthcare Philanthropy: Relationship-Based Leadership

What stands out about Allie’s leadership is her belief that strong organizations are built through relationships by investing in people, creating clarity of purpose, and empowering teams to do their best work. Allie shares what she’s most proud of, how you get people to come together, bring their best ideas, and how you build a structure around them that allows those ideas to become reality when it comes to health system innovation. To be perfectly honest, I’m not entirely sure, Doug. As CEOs, we hopefully have the skills of a diplomat. We’ve got the imagination of story writers. KGH Foundation: As CEOs, we need to have the skills of a diplomat and the imagination of story writers. It’s this constant drip of influence and inspiration.

Where we have the structures, and we have the relationships, and we have the influence, and whether it’s standing up a small innovation center or if it’s building a health building that provides the services or space for the services that the government cannot yet get to, if it’s convening communities.
