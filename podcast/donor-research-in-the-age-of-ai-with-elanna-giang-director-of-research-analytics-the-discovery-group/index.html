Donor Research In The Age Of AI With Elanna Giang, Director Of Research & Analytics, The Discovery Group

August 14, 2026 · Hosted by Douglas Nelson

Nonprofit leaders often struggle to identify and engage the right supporters, leaving valuable philanthropic opportunities untapped. Building sustainable donor growth requires a grounded approach to prospect research that goes far deeper than net worth or basic online search results. In this episode, Elanna Giang, Director of Research and Analytics at The Discovery Group, shares actionable insights on how fundraising teams can transform raw data into meaningful relationships. She explores the practical applications and hidden dangers of AI in donor identification, why values alignment outweighs financial capacity, and how involving researchers in early strategy sessions empowers organizations to connect with confidence.

Breaking Silos: Involving Researchers In High-Level Donor Strategy

Maybe do some of that, but what it comes down to is the number of people that you can connect with and your work and the work of prospect researchers helps create that shared language and that comfort for organizations to more confidently and more consistently reach out to their donors. One of the other pieces that you’ve underlined in the conversations with the team here, and I know I’ve seen you do it with clients, is making sure and advocating for bringing prospect researchers into the donor strategy conversation. Why is that so important?

I like to think of fundraising as a fact-finding mission almost. I think about a lot of these things in a discovery way. There’s an ongoing process of discovery and fundraising. Again, people are always changing.

Including the key members of your team as early as you can is only going to create a more multifaceted approach to understanding and engaging with a donor. If you have more people in the room who maybe some are more curious than others, that can only help build a more multifaceted fundraising strategy.
