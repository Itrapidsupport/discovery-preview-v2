Questions, answered

Do you only work with nonprofits?+

Yes. The Discovery Group is a consulting firm dedicated to the non-profit sector, which we call social profit.

Can one engagement span several practices?+

Yes. We integrate campaign counsel, governance, search and coaching so the right combination of skills shows up for each organization.

Where do you work?+

Our home base is in Vancouver, BC, with an office in Toronto, and we support clients across Canada and beyond.

Ready to discuss your organization’s needs?

From strategy to leadership transitions, we support organizations like yours across Canada.
